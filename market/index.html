Understanding the numbers

Average selling prices reflect the mix of properties sold and are not individual home valuations. Year-over-year (YoY) compares the same month last year; month-over-month (MoM) compares the previous month.

All prices are in Canadian dollars. A dash indicates missing data. No values are estimated or forecast.
