Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: November 4, 2026. This policy describes information handling on Raymond Chen’s real estate website. For questions or requests, contact raymondc7865@gmail.com or call 647-879-7865.

Information you choose to provide

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional phone number, preferred contact method, real estate needs, property preferences, area, budget, timeline, and any message you provide. We use these details to respond to your inquiry and provide the real estate assistance you request. Please avoid sending identity documents, payment details, or other sensitive information through this form.

Consent and contact

The form includes a contact permission choice. Submitting an inquiry does not automatically subscribe you to a marketing newsletter. Separate external sign-up services may request their own consent. You can ask to change your contact preferences or stop further contact at any time.

Google Forms and other services

The Contact page embeds the live Google Form, so its questions and choices reflect the form’s current version. When you submit it, your response is sent directly to Google Forms for Raymond Chen to review. Google processes information under its own privacy terms, and information may be processed outside Canada. Read Google’s Privacy Policy. Links to property listings, sign-up services, social platforms and other websites open services with their own privacy practices.

Market data and browser storage

The Market page presents TRREB market statistics and stores the most recently loaded figures in your browser to keep the charts usable if an update is unavailable. Clearing your browser’s site data removes its stored copy.

Hosting and analytics

The hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as request and network information. This version does not add advertising trackers or a third-party analytics script. The embedded Google Form loads Google content on the Contact page and may use cookies or similar technologies under Google’s terms. Other external services may use cookies when you visit or submit information to them.

Access, correction and retention

You may contact Raymond to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your inquiry information, or to withdraw consent, subject to applicable obligations. Inquiry information should be kept only as needed to respond to your request, provide requested services, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Updates

This policy may be revised when the website’s services or information practices change. The updated date above identifies the current version.
