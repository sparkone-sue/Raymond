Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: November 7, 2026. This policy explains how inquiry information is handled on Raymond Chen’s real estate website. For privacy questions, contact raymondc7865@gmail.com or 647-879-7865.

Information you provide

The Contact page uses a website-designed inquiry form to collect your name, email address, phone number, area of real estate interest, message, and your consent to be contacted. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and provide requested real estate assistance. Please do not include identity documents, financial account numbers, or other sensitive information in your message.

How inquiries are submitted

When you submit the form on this website, its information is transmitted to a Google Form used to receive and manage inquiries. The form is displayed in the website’s own design rather than embedded as a Google Forms interface. Google may process data outside Canada. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Consent and communications

By checking the consent box, you agree to be contacted about your inquiry using the contact information you provide. Submitting the form does not by itself subscribe you to a marketing newsletter. You may request that we stop contacting you.

Website hosting and external links

This site is published using GitHub Pages. The hosting service may process technical data needed to deliver and secure the website. Links to listings, social networks and other external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Market information and browser storage

Market charts may store recently loaded market figures in your browser to support display when a data update is unavailable. You can clear this information using your browser’s site-data controls.

Access, correction and retention

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your inquiry information, contact Raymond using the details above. Requests are subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. Inquiry information is retained only as needed for its intended purpose and applicable obligations.

Policy updates

This policy may change when the website or its information practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
