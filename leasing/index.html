02 / TENANTS

Find a place to call home.

Understand your budget, prepare rental application documents and review the lease before signing. Clarify utilities, insurance, condo rules and move-in arrangements where applicable.

Residential rental rules in Ontario depend on the type of accommodation and the Residential Tenancies Act.

03 / MOVE-IN GUIDE

A practical move-in checklist.

✓Lease & PaymentsConfirm the signed lease, rent and lawful deposits.
✓UtilitiesArrange utility accounts and confirm responsibilities.
✓InsuranceReview any insurance requirements.
✓Condo RegistrationBook the elevator and arrange building access, if needed.
✓Condition RecordDocument the property condition with photos.
✓Keys & AddressCheck keys, fobs, contacts and address updates.
