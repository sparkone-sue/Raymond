03 / COSTS

Plan beyond the purchase price.

The purchase price is only one part of the transaction. Understand the expenses that may arise before closing.

Land Transfer TaxOntario land transfer tax generally applies; Toronto also charges municipal land transfer tax.
Legal Fees & DisbursementsLawyer fees, title searches, registration and closing documents.
Title InsuranceCoverage for specified title-related risks, often arranged by your lawyer.
Home InspectionAn optional or agreement-dependent assessment of property condition.
AdjustmentsProperty taxes and certain prepaid expenses may be adjusted on closing.
Mortgage CostsAppraisal, default insurance and lender-specific charges may apply.

05 / CLOSING

Prepare for closing day.

Once an offer is accepted, coordinate with your lawyer, lender and insurer to prepare for ownership transfer.

Before closing

Confirm financing and outstanding conditions; review legal documents; arrange insurance and utilities; prepare closing funds.

After closing

Confirm services, update your address, review tax or condo payment arrangements and retain closing documents.

06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between a deposit and a down payment?

The deposit is typically paid under the purchase agreement and credited toward the price on closing. The down payment is the buyer-funded portion of the purchase price.

What closing costs should I budget for?

Consider land transfer taxes, legal fees, title insurance, adjustments and transaction-specific expenses.

Do I need a lawyer?

A real estate lawyer handles title, registration and other legal requirements for closing.

How long does a transaction take?

Timelines vary based on market conditions, financing, negotiations and the agreed closing date.
